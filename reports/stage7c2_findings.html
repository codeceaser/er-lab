Stage 7C.2 asked one question: does an authority-aware Wiki — deterministic, or enriched by a bounded LLM — recover cross-document evidence that flat Vector retrieval misses? The experiment was built so that it could say no. It said no.

The mechanism

Where every evidence slot went

Each question returns exactly K source chunks — the same budget Vector gets. The frozen final-K policy fills Tier 1 first with the chunks that established the traversed path, then Tier 2 with everything else reached, ranked by query cosine. What the four drops have in common

On Q05, Q06, Q07 and Q10 the required evidence was reached — it sat in the candidate set. It lost its slot to a chunk that established the path rather than answered the question. Vector spends all K slots on query relevance and keeps those facts; the Wiki spends most of them proving how it got there.

This is not an unanticipated defect. The frozen contract predicted it in advance — “one wrong hop costs one required fact” at zero slack — and named it the price of hub navigation. Stage 7C.2 measured how often the price is charged.

Attribution

Which layer earned its complexity

The experiment was restructured specifically so this question could be answered. Three deltas, each isolating one variable.

W1-D vs D0 marginal value of LLM semantic seed enrichment Worse on 1 question. No value added.
W1-FULL vs W1-D marginal value of claim-derived routing Materially equivalent — identical on every axis, every question.
W1-FULL vs D0 total value of the LLM-assisted Wiki Worse on 1 question. The model layer added nothing measurable.
Why the third card is the only one that licenses a conclusion

An earlier revision of this experiment compared the claim-routing arm against a baseline that also consumed the compiler’s output. That design could show whether claims helped routing, but it could never show whether the compiler was needed — it was seeding both sides. D0 exists to close that gap, and only W1-FULL vs D0 supports a statement about whether the model-derived layer was necessary at all.

Twenty claim-derived links removed; every outcome and every coverage figure unchanged. Deterministic source anchors absorbed each hop. The connectivity the claims were meant to provide was already there without them — which is what the redesigned architecture predicted, and is weak-positive evidence by construction rather than a triumph.

Price

The dollar figure is trivial. The dependency is not: the W1 representation cannot be rebuilt without a human re-judging every claim, alias and summary. That recurring cost is charged against W1-FULL vs D0 — the total — not against the routing delta alone, because pricing a layer against a benefit it only partly produces understates what it is really for.

Boundaries

What this does not establish

  • The corpus cannot test hub fan-out at scale. Six documents, eleven single-chunk revisions, one cross-document phrase anchor. Low branching here is a property of the corpus, not evidence that Wiki navigation behaves well on an enterprise graph. No scaling claim follows.
  • Wiki extraction is not shown to beat Graph extraction. The frozen Graph figures (0.80 recall, 0.86 precision) and the frozen Wiki figures (0.87, 0.88) are one non-deterministic snapshot each, on one small corpus, with only approximately aligned definitions.
  • Gate A was unreachable before this stage began — Stage 7C.1’s Gate Q failed. Nothing measured here could have rescued it, and nothing here was allowed to weaken it.
  • The architecture decision is not made here. Gate B and Gate C both remain live, and both depend on an owner page-quality rating that is deliberately not self-scored.
Where it stands

On the measured evidence, Gate B is the better-supported reading: W1-FULL shows no qualifying improvement over D0, so the compiler’s incremental value is not merely unjustified by cost — it is not measurable on this corpus. But Gate C is not ruled out, because D0 also trails Vector on four questions, so the deterministic Wiki has not demonstrated retrieval value either. The blind six-page quality packet is the remaining input.
